07 — Two-parameter bifurcation analysis (checklist 3.7)¶

Convert validated one-parameter special points from Notebooks 05 (TTX) and 06 (ouabain / complete-TTX) into bifurcation curves in the biological plane

$$ (\alpha_I,\alpha_P)\in[0,1]^2. $$

  • Primary continuation plane: $(\alpha_I,\alpha_P)$
  • PyCont types: H-C2 (Hopf) and LP-C (equilibrium fold)
  • Secondary $(g_I,I_{\mathrm{ep}})$ figure: linear transform only (no second continuation)
  • Codimension-2 labels reported only when the installed software detects them

Out of scope: individual Jacobian / first-Lyapunov coefficient project (Notes/MATH3888_individual_component_jacobian_hopf_plan.md); automatic two-parameter LPC / HOM campaigns.

Environment: continuation engine scripts/run_07_two_parameter_bifurcation.py is intended for Docker (curuy/math3888:latest). This notebook loads saved CSVs/figures; set RERUN_CONTINUATION = True only if you intentionally want a full recompute.

1. Aim and relation to one-parameter slices¶

Notebook 05 found a biological equilibrium fold LP_bio on the TTX-only slice $\alpha_P=1$ at $\alpha_I\approx 0.614$, and no biological Hopf on $\alpha_I\in[0,1]$.

Notebook 06 found, on the complete-TTX slice $\alpha_I=0$, two Hopfs on one slow-orbit skeleton: H_bio at $\alpha_P\approx 0.268$ and H_ext at $\alpha_P\approx 1.48$. Ouabain alone ($\alpha_I=1$) had no biological Hopf/LP on the untreated-connected EP-C branch.

This notebook asks how those landmarks organise as curves in $(\alpha_I,\alpha_P)$, and whether they meet at software-detected codimension-2 points.

3. Verify $\alpha_I$ / $\alpha_P$ mappings¶

Analysis-level scalings (canonical ODEs unchanged):

$$ g_I = \alpha_I\, G_{I0},\qquad I_{\mathrm{ep}} = \alpha_P\, I_{EP0}, $$

with $G_{I0}=4$ and $I_{EP0}=-0.22$. Joint ContClass helper build_alpha_IP_vode_system freezes baseline $g_I$ and $I_{\mathrm{ep}}$ and places both $\alpha$ factors in the voltage equation.

5. Two-parameter continuation methodology¶

Installed PyCont types (Lab 7 / ContClass.curve_list):

Purpose type freepars Codim-2 if requested
Hopf curve H-C2 ['alpha_I','alpha_P'] BT, ZH, GH (DH not for H-C2)
LP / fold curve LP-C ['alpha_I','alpha_P'] BT, ZH, CP

Workflow used by the engine:

  1. Build joint $\alpha$ VODE system.
  2. Short EP-C on $\alpha_I=0$ (recover H1/H2) and on $\alpha_P=1$ (recover LP1).
  3. H-C2 from each Hopf, forward and backward.
  4. LP-C from LP_bio, forward and backward.
  5. Deduplicate overlapping segments / special points.
  6. Cross-validate against NB05/06 anchors before interpretation.

6–8. Hopf continuation, deduplication, and comparison¶

Both complete-TTX Hopfs were continued with H-C2. The two seeds meet outside the biological square near $(\alpha_I,\alpha_P)\approx(-2.91,\,1.33)$ (sample distance $\approx 0.004$), so they belong to one physical Hopf curve. Inside $[0,1]^2$ the biologically relevant arc is the branch through H_bio; the H_ext arc lies at $\alpha_P>1$ (mathematical exterior).

10. Codimension-2 special-point summary¶

On H-C2, LocBifPoints included BT, ZH, GH. On LP-C, BT, ZH, CP.

Detected in this run: two unique ZH (zero-Hopf / fold–Hopf) points on the LP curve, both outside $[0,1]^2$:

  • near $(\alpha_I,\alpha_P)\approx(1.12,\,3.97)$
  • near $(\alpha_I,\alpha_P)\approx(0.345,\,-0.184)$

Not detected: GH, BT, CP.

Do not invent GH/BT/CP from visual curve crossings inside the square. A nearby H–LP crossing without a software label remains an observation, not a claimed Bogdanov–Takens point.

15. Interpretation for TTX / ouabain / combined drugs¶

  1. TTX fold as pump changes. LP_bio continues as an LP curve through the biological square: the fold persists as $\alpha_P$ decreases, shifting mildly in $\alpha_I$. Combined blockade can cross this fold boundary.

  2. Complete-TTX Hopf as TTX is relaxed. From H_bio, the Hopf curve moves to larger $\alpha_I$ while $\alpha_P$ falls, leaving the square near $\alpha_P\approx 0$ around $\alpha_I\approx 0.57$. It does not reach the ouabain-alone edge $\alpha_I=1$ inside $[0,1]$ — consistent with NB06 having no biological Hopf on that slice.

  3. Why Hopf at $\alpha_I=0$ but not at $\alpha_I=1$. Geometry: the Hopf curve intersects $\alpha_I=0$ twice (H_bio, H_ext) but only H_bio is in $[0,1]$; the same curve never crosses $\alpha_I=1$ with $\alpha_P\in[0,1]$.

  4. Combined-drug path. Decreasing $\alpha_P$ at $\alpha_I=0$ crosses H_bio (unstable→stable), matching NB03/NB06. Paths with partial TTX may instead meet the LP curve depending on dose combination.

  5. Codim-2. Only exterior ZH points were labelled; no GH/BT/CP inside the drug square. Visual H–LP proximity inside the square is not claimed as BT.

16. Numerical limitations¶

  • H-C2 / LP-C are not AUTO-gated; still run in Docker for stack parity with NB05/06.
  • Forward/backward and multi-seed continuations can retrace the same branch; dedup is required.
  • MX is numerical termination, not a bifurcation.
  • Period / LC structure is not re-continued here; GH→LPC would be a focused follow-up if GH appeared.
  • Stability samples use accepted current-balance roots; multiple equilibria can coexist near folds.
