01 — Untreated 7D Plant–Kim baseline¶

Baseline implementation of the untreated Plant–Kim (1976) R15 model.

  • Equations/parameters: Notes/model.md (Plant–Kim 1976 Tables I–II, with Canavier et al. 1990 typographical corrections to $\alpha_m$, $\beta_m$, $\beta_h$)
  • Reusable code: src/plant_kim_model.py
  • Solver: PyDSTool VODE only
  • Kernel: Python 3.9 (PyDSTool) (pydstool)

Implementation choices (not tabulated in Plant–Kim):

  • I_ext = 0 (untreated spontaneous activity)
  • Initial condition: V(0) = -50 mV with gates at steady state at V(0)

Audit note: the printed Table I forms for $\alpha_m$ (denominator), $\beta_m$, and $\beta_h$ are inconsistent with the HH voltage transform; the implementation uses the Canavier (1990) corrections. Pre-correction figures are preserved under outputs/figures/baseline/before_audit/.

3. Gating-function diagnostics¶

Activation/inactivation should generally lie in $[0,1]$; voltage-dependent $\tau$ should be positive and finite over $V\in[-80,40]$ mV.

Removable singularities in $\alpha_m$ ($V=-26/1.2$) and $\alpha_n$ ($V=-21/1.21$) are handled by analytic limits in the NumPy helpers (expm1 / branch).

windows = [(0, 5000), (5000, 15000), (15000, 40000), (20000, 40000)]
print('Window summaries for V(t):')
for a, b in windows:
    m = (t >= a) & (t <= b)
    vv = V[m]
    print(f'  [{a:5.0f}, {b:5.0f}] ms: Vmin={vv.min():8.4g}  Vmax={vv.max():8.4g}  mean={vv.mean():8.4g}')

late = t >= 10000
V_late = V[late]
t_late = t[late]
n_spike = int(np.sum((V_late[:-1] <= -20) & (V_late[1:] > -20)))
print('upward crossings of -20 mV after t=10 s (spike proxy):', n_spike)

# group spikes into bursts (gap > 1.5 s)
cross = np.where((V_late[:-1] <= -20) & (V_late[1:] > -20))[0]
if len(cross):
    tspike = t_late[cross]
    groups = []
    cur = [tspike[0]]
    for i in range(1, len(tspike)):
        if tspike[i] - tspike[i - 1] > 1500:
            groups.append(cur)
            cur = [tspike[i]]
        else:
            cur.append(tspike[i])
    groups.append(cur)
    sizes = [len(g) for g in groups]
    periods = [groups[i + 1][0] - groups[i][0] for i in range(len(groups) - 1)]
    print(f'late bursts: {len(groups)}; spikes/burst={sizes}')
    if periods:
        print(f'burst periods (s): {[round(p/1000, 3) for p in periods]}; median={np.median(periods)/1000:.3f} s')

9. Qualitative assessment¶

Expected: untreated normal R15 bursting (spike clusters separated by hyperpolarized intervals).

BEFORE audit (literal printed $\alpha_m$ denom / $\beta_m$/$\beta_h$): slow oscillation $\sim 10$–$11$ s, no spikes, pathological $S_I$/$\tau_{X_I}$, $X_I\sim 10^{-6}$. Figures: outputs/figures/baseline/before_audit/.

AFTER Canavier (1990) Table I typographical corrections: untreated VODE trajectory shows fast spikes on a slow envelope. After discarding the initial transient ($\sim 10$ s):

  • slow / burst period $\approx 9$ s
  • $\approx 4$ spikes per burst
  • $V$ range roughly $-52$ to $+27$ mV

Qualitatively consistent with Plant–Kim untreated bursting. Spike count per burst is closer to Canavier et al. (1990) at $I_{\mathrm{stim}}=0$ (they report 4 vs Plant–Kim’s printed 7) than to the original figure’s count — no parameter retuning was performed.
